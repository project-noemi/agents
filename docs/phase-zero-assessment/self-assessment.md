# Phase 0 Self-Assessment (Self-Serve Front Door)

This is a short, self-scored questionnaire an SME completes **before** a full Phase 0 engagement. It takes about 10–15 minutes and needs no assessor present.

Its purpose is not to grade you. It is to:

- help you decide whether you are ready to start the full assessment now or fix a few things first
- pre-qualify the engagement so time is not spent on a workflow that is not ready
- focus the assessor-led work on the areas that actually need attention

This does **not** replace the full assessment. It is a fast first look. The full [ai-readiness-assessment.md](ai-readiness-assessment.md) and [security-assessment.md](security-assessment.md) go much deeper.

## How To Score

Answer each question with one of:

- **Ready** — this is true today, and you could show evidence
- **Partial** — this is partly true, or true for some of the workflow but not all
- **Not yet** — this is not true today, or you are not sure

Answer for **one specific workflow** you want AI to help with, not the whole company. Be honest — an inflated score just wastes the first assessment session.

## Part 1 — Business Fit (pick one workflow)

1. There is a specific, repetitive workflow that is slow, high-volume, or painful, and a named person feels that pain.
   `Ready | Partial | Not yet`

2. Someone owns this workflow and can make decisions about changing it.
   `Ready | Partial | Not yet`

3. Leadership supports trying AI here and will give people time to test it.
   `Ready | Partial | Not yet`

## Part 2 — Workflow And Data

4. The workflow has repeatable steps with reasonably clear inputs and outputs (it is not different every time).
   `Ready | Partial | Not yet`

5. The documents, systems, or knowledge the work relies on are known and accessible.
   `Ready | Partial | Not yet`

6. We can tell the difference between a normal case and an exception that a human must handle.
   `Ready | Partial | Not yet`

## Part 3 — Control And Measurement

7. It is clear which steps a human must approve, especially anything customer-facing or risky.
   `Ready | Partial | Not yet`

8. We know, at least roughly, the current volume, turnaround time, and effort this work takes today.
   `Ready | Partial | Not yet`

9. We could agree on what a "good" result looks like before starting.
   `Ready | Partial | Not yet`

## Part 4 — People And Attitude

These mirror the Individual Adoption Readiness area of the full assessment. Answer for the people who would actually do the work, not just yourself.

10. The people doing this work understand what AI can and cannot reliably do.
    `Ready | Partial | Not yet`

11. Those people feel reasonably confident using AI in their own work, not anxious or exposed.
    `Ready | Partial | Not yet`

12. Concerns like fear of replacement or distrust of AI output have been talked about openly, not left unspoken.
    `Ready | Partial | Not yet`

13. The team is open to changing *how* the work is done, not just doing the old way faster.
    `Ready | Partial | Not yet`

## Part 5 — Safety Basics

14. Multi-factor authentication is on, and we know who can access the systems this workflow touches.
    `Ready | Partial | Not yet`

15. Sensitive data that must never go into an AI tool has been identified.
    `Ready | Partial | Not yet`

## Reading Your Result

Count your answers. This is a rough signal, not a verdict:

- **Mostly Ready** — you are likely a strong candidate. The full assessment can move quickly and focus on designing the pilot.
- **A mix of Ready and Partial** — you are probably ready to start, but the assessment should concentrate on your `Partial` and `Not yet` areas first.
- **Several Not yet, especially in Parts 1, 3, or 5** — worth closing a few basics before the full engagement. Any `Not yet` in Part 5 (safety basics) should be looked at early regardless of the rest.

Bring this completed sheet to the first session. It tells the assessor where to spend time and where not to.
