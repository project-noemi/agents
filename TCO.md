TEAM-LEVEL TCO AND PROCESS ROI: AI-ASSISTED SUPPORT TICKET HANDLING

Scenario: An 8-person customer support team handles 3,200 tickets per month. The AI routes each ticket by policy (category, urgency, data sensitivity) and drafts a reply, and a human agent reviews every outgoing message. All figures are illustrative estimates in EUR, excluding VAT. Loaded labor cost is 28 EUR/h for support agents and 50 EUR/h for engineering and security work.

ITEMIZED TCO

Licenses and usage: AI licenses: 8 seats x 30 EUR/month = 240 EUR/month (2,880 EUR in Year 1). API usage for routing and drafting: about 3,200 tickets x 0.05 EUR = 150 EUR/month (1,800 EUR in Year 1).

Integration (one-time, 128 hours x 50 EUR = 6,400 EUR): Policy-based routing rules: 24 h = 1,200 EUR. Helpdesk API, webhooks and knowledge base connection: 40 h = 2,000 EUR. Security controls (PII redaction, audit log, access control): 24 h = 1,200 EUR. Pilot and testing: 20 h = 1,000 EUR. Documentation and project management: 20 h = 1,000 EUR.

Training: Initial training: 8 people x 6 h x 28 EUR = 1,344 EUR (one-time). Trainer preparation: 16 h x 50 EUR = 800 EUR (one-time). Refresher training: 8 people x 2 h per quarter x 28 EUR = about 149 EUR/month (1,792 EUR in Year 1).

Governance: Rule tuning and monthly Blue Team review: 6 h/month x 50 EUR = 300 EUR/month (3,600 EUR in Year 1).

TCO totals: One-time cost: 8,544 EUR. Recurring cost: about 839 EUR/month. Year-1 TCO: 18,616 EUR.

BEFORE VS. AFTER AI (SUPPORT TICKET HANDLING)

First response time (median): 4.5 h before, 0.5 h after (-89%). Full resolution time (median): 26 h before, 15 h after (-42%). Agent handling time per ticket: 14.0 min before, 9.5 min after (-32%). Of which triage and routing: about 3.0 min (manual) before, about 0.5 min (automatic by policy) after. Rework and reopened tickets: 6.0% before, 3.5% after.

RISK-ADJUSTED MONTHLY RETURN

Time saved: 3,200 tickets x 4.5 min = 240 h x 28 EUR = 6,720 EUR gross. With a 70% confidence factor (freed time is only partly turned into real capacity) this is 4,704 EUR. Fewer errors: 80 fewer rework cases (6.0% to 3.5%) x 30 EUR = 2,400 EUR gross. With a 75% confidence factor this is 1,800 EUR. The 30 EUR per case is about 12 EUR of agent time plus about 18 EUR of goodwill, credits and escalations. Security risk avoided (Blue Teaming): the yearly chance of a reportable data incident falls from 8.0% (ad hoc AI use, no controls) to 2.5% (managed deployment). Assumed incident cost is 60,000 EUR (GDPR response, legal, notification, staff time, lost customers). So (8.0% - 2.5%) x 60,000 / 12 = 275 EUR/month gross. With a 50% confidence factor this is 138 EUR. The controls are routing sensitive tickets (payment, health, ID) to a human-only queue, PII redaction before the model, prompt-injection test cases, audit logs and a monthly Blue Team log review. Their cost is included in the TCO.

Total gross benefit: 9,395 EUR/month. Risk-adjusted benefit: 6,642 EUR/month. Run cost: 839 EUR/month. Final risk-adjusted net monthly return at full ramp: 5,803 EUR/month.

YEAR-1 RESULT

Ramp-up: month 1 is build (0% benefit), month 2 delivers 40%, month 3 delivers 70%, then 100%. That equals 10.1 full-benefit months. Year-1 benefit: 67,084 EUR. Year-1 TCO: 18,616 EUR. Year-1 net return: 48,468 EUR (ROI 260%). Payback: about 3.7 months. Break-even needs only about 1,843 EUR/month of benefit, which is 28% of the estimate. Downside case (only 50% of benefits appear): net 2,482 EUR/month at full ramp, Year-1 net 14,926 EUR (ROI 80%), payback about 7 months.

Note: time savings turn into cash only if the freed hours are used (more tickets handled, less overtime, an avoided hire).
